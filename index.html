Finance faculty · Researcher · Jaipur, India

Dr. Vinay Khandelwal

I teach finance to managers who will make real capital decisions, and I research how markets price risk.

I am an Assistant Professor of Finance at ICFAI Business School, Jaipur, and adjunct faculty at Eaton Business School in the UAE. My courses range from Financial Management and Derivatives to Mergers and Acquisitions, Power BI and Introduction to AI.

I believe finance is learned by doing. So my classes use simulations and the tools students will meet at work, such as Bloomberg, Power BI and Python.

My research asks how markets price risk. It covers asset pricing, ESG, digital transformation and the metaverse in banking. I hold a doctorate in management from Jaipuria Institute of Management and lead an ICSSR-funded project of INR 15 lakh.
