Why this approach works

Practice and data beat lectures and opinion.

1.5×

Students in lecture-only courses were 1.5 times more likely to fail than students in courses with active learning. This comes from an analysis of 225 studies in science, technology, engineering and mathematics.

Freeman et al., PNAS, 2014 ↗
5–6%

Firms that made decisions based on data produced 5–6% more output and productivity than their other investments predicted. The study covered 179 large public companies.

Brynjolfsson, Hitt and Kim, 2011 ↗
